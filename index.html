Good morning, it's Saturday, November 28, 2026. The U.S.–Iran confrontation leads the news again: the Journal reports the U.S. military struck and disabled a commercial vessel in the Gulf of Oman, the latest episode in Iran's efforts to slip past an American naval blockade that has squeezed its economy.
